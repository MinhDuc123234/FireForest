How accurate is it?

Tested on 2019+ data the method never saw. Aqua observed is the truth.

Trend, Vietnam 2003–2025

Correction factor R

Aqua observed ÷ physics prediction. 1 = physics exact; 0.5 = it over-counted two times.

Aqua is drifting after 2022

Median local overpass hour. It was about 13.3 h until 2022, so calibration uses data up to 2022 only.

Limits

  • A hotspot is not burned area; some are crop-residue burning.
  • FIRMS has no cloud mask; clouds are handled only on average.
  • Detection-limit constants are approximations, so R is far from 1.
  • Province bands reflect method uncertainty, not local sampling noise.
